# Project Summary

SmartEstimate is a modern SaaS platform designed to simplify, accelerate, and improve the process of creating professional construction estimates.

The platform combines structured construction knowledge, intelligent automation, and artificial intelligence to help contractors prepare accurate estimates in minutes instead of hours.

The first target market is Ukraine, focusing on Kyiv and the Kyiv region.

The long-term goal is to become an international platform supporting multiple countries, languages, and construction standards.

---

# Vision

SmartEstimate is not just an estimate calculator.

It is a construction knowledge platform that helps professionals throughout the entire estimating process.

The platform combines:

• Construction Knowledge

• Business Logic

• Artificial Intelligence

• Modern User Experience

into one ecosystem.

# Development Strategy

The project will be developed incrementally.

Every feature must be production-ready before moving to the next milestone.

The MVP will focus on estimate creation.

Artificial Intelligence will be introduced gradually after the core platform is complete.
