# Product Vision

## Mission

SmartEstimate aims to transform the way construction estimates are created by combining construction expertise, structured knowledge, and artificial intelligence into a single modern platform.

The goal is to reduce the time required to prepare professional estimates from hours to minutes while improving accuracy, consistency, and transparency.

---

## Vision

SmartEstimate is not intended to become just another estimate calculator.

The long-term vision is to build the most intelligent construction estimating platform that acts as an experienced estimator and project assistant.

Instead of simply calculating prices, SmartEstimate understands construction processes, recommends missing work, calculates materials, analyzes market prices, and assists contractors throughout the entire project lifecycle.

---

## Core Philosophy

Construction estimating should not depend on human memory.

The platform should:

- remember everything;
- calculate everything;
- recommend improvements;
- prevent mistakes;
- explain every recommendation.

Artificial Intelligence should support the contractor, not replace their decisions.

The final decision always belongs to the user.

# Non Goals

It is NOT intended to perform structural engineering calculations.

Its purpose is estimating and project preparation.
